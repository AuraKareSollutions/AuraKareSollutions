Security Framework

Protection is built into every part of the process.

Physical & Operational Controls

Careful access creates the first layer of confidence.

Our working environments use verified identity, monitored entry, clear desk habits, and firm limits on removable media.

  • Photo identification and proximity access
  • Monitored production areas
  • Authorized-visitor procedures

Data Protection Controls

Information stays protected wherever it moves.

Technical safeguards reduce exposure during preparation, transfer, storage, review, and final disposition.

  • Credential rules, encryption, and firewalls
  • Private network transmission
  • Separated handling for sensitive files

Employee Confidentiality

Privacy becomes part of everyday behavior.

Teams receive clear expectations, practical training, and ongoing oversight before handling client information.

  • Confidentiality commitments
  • Pre-employment screening
  • Security awareness refreshers

Continuity Planning

Prepared response keeps essential work moving.

AuraKare Sollutions plans for interruptions so teams can restore priority workflows quickly and keep clients informed throughout recovery.

  • Communication interruption procedures
  • Backup power and connectivity planning
  • Prioritized restoration playbooks

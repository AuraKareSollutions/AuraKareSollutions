Privacy Policy

At AuraKare Sollutions, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information in connection with our website, client communications, and service delivery.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information when you contact us, request a consultation, submit a form, or engage with our services. This may include your name, company name, job title, email address, phone number, location, and any other details you provide in your message or project request.

We may also collect limited technical information automatically through our website, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and usage trends that help us improve the user experience.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested services
  • Manage client projects, communications, and service delivery
  • Improve the quality and relevance of our offerings
  • Send operational updates or relevant information related to your project or request
  • Maintain compliance with legal, contractual, and regulatory obligations

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share personal data only when necessary to deliver services, support our operations, meet legal requirements, or protect our rights, property, or the safety of our clients and employees.

Examples may include trusted third-party service providers, professional advisors, and other parties acting under confidentiality obligations and only to the extent required for the relevant purpose.

4. Data Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to help protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. While no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, we take appropriate precautions to reduce risk and maintain the integrity of the information entrusted to us.

5. Cookies and Cookie Choices

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use essential browser storage to remember your cookie choice and support core website functions. These technologies do not identify you by themselves.

5.1 Optional cookies

Optional analytics or marketing cookies are not activated unless you choose to allow them through the cookie-consent banner. If enabled, Google Analytics 4 may process usage information such as pages visited, device type, approximate location, and traffic source. You can reject unnecessary cookies or change your choices at any time using the Cookie Settings button.

5.2 Your consent

When you select Accept all, Reject unnecessary, or Save preferences, we store your choice in your browser. You can also clear this choice by deleting your browser’s site data. The website remains available when optional cookies are rejected.

5.3 Cookie categories

  • Essential: required for core site functions and always enabled.
  • Analytics: used to understand visits and improve the website, only if enabled.
  • Marketing: used for campaign measurement or personalization, only if enabled.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it where appropriate.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable local law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of your personal information. If you would like to update your preferences or ask questions about your data, please contact us using the details below.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us at hello@aurakaresollutions.com.

9. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. Any revised version will be posted on this page and will become effective as of the date stated above or the date of publication.
